## 2. Where each limit now lives

1. **Not a first-ten record:** “One division makes the evidence's scale and timing concrete…” now carries the estimate, missing delivery detail, later observation counts and evidentiary threshold together. The arithmetic paragraph is the right host because it introduces the inference that might otherwise be mistaken for a documented first-ten cohort.

2. **Company-wide figures:** “Read the customer counts rather than the money…” now defines exactly what both sets of figures measure. It is the right host because it tells the reader how—and how far—to interpret the ladder.

3. **Interrupted series:** “When the numbers resumed in the good months of November and December…” now qualifies the evidentiary value at the moment the series is presented. The qualification therefore travels with the claim that the grind’s shape is visible.

4. **Pre-existing audience:** “Norris says the opposite…” now makes the audience asset part of the account of worked-for acquisition. This is the right host because the asset qualifies the implied reproducibility and speed of that process.

5. **Interested source:** “Written at the time by a founder with an interest in the outcome…” now carries both the advantage of contemporaneous reporting and the weakness of seller testimony. The source note is the natural host for that evidentiary judgment.

## 3. What the chapter loses

The chapter loses a conspicuous, easily scanned audit of its reservations; readers must now register each one where the relevant claim is made.
