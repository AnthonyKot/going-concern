**2. Hosts**

- **(A)** lives in the sentence that interprets the case — "the amount people freely chose failed the project's own requirement." That is the exact moment a reader is tempted to treat the pledge as a price signal, and the word "freely" is what invites the mistake, so the qualification is attached to it rather than appearing five sections later.
- **(B)** lives in "The result is a floor." The claim being qualified is that the calculation produces a floor; the incompleteness, the review date and Part III are now conditions of that result, and the narrowed purpose ("visible direct costs already exceed it") replaces the unqualified "below it." The full list of omissions is kept, not summarised.
- **(C)** lives in "A discount is a new offer, not an eraser." That is the paragraph where the reader reaches for a lower number after a no; the four meanings of "expensive" and the chapter-eight rule are placed as the gate on that reflex, before the permitted ways of discounting are listed.

None of the three needed to stand alone; each had a sentence already making the claim it qualifies.

**3. What the chapter loses**

The clean, quotable checklist of three caveats that a reader could find in one place — the reservations are now only encountered by reading the argument through, and the reader who skips to Monday will meet the review date on the price card without the sentence explaining why it is there.
