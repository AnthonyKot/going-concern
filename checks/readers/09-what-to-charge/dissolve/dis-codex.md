2. New hosts

- **(A):** “Studio Neat cannot fill that role because…” is the right host because the counter-case paragraph is where the chapter defines what the campaign cannot establish about durable retail pricing.

- **(B):** “The result is a provisional floor…” qualifies the sentence that creates the floor, making its omissions, immediate purpose, review date and later fuller calculation part of the method itself.

- **(C):** “A discount is a new offer…” is the right host because the risk of misreading an objection matters precisely when the seller is considering lowering the number.

3. What the chapter loses
