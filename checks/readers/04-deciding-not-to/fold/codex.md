1. Placement

Boundary one should follow: “Expect to want to move it. That wanting is data.” This is where the fixed date is presented as decisive, and where a thoughtful reader first asks whether missing it condemns the business or merely reflects the operator’s incomplete skills.

Boundary two should follow: “The test is independence, not unfamiliarity.” This is where the chapter finishes defining acceptable customer evidence; the immediate objection is that even a sound test proves nothing if the operator has not actually gathered that evidence.

3. Other required changes

None. The chapter contains no forward reference to the Boundaries section; its heading and introductory sentence can simply be removed with the relocated text.

4. What is lost

Only the useful visual signal that these are two explicit limits; their force and their effect on the argument are preserved.
