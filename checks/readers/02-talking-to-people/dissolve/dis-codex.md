## 2. Where each limit now lives

1. **Evidence for diagnosis versus cure:** No insertion is needed. “The counter-case this chapter does not have” already says that the method lacks a checkable test, that its failure mode is reasoned rather than demonstrated, and that the evidential debt remains.

2. **Demand without precedent:** The revised opening sentence of “The existing workaround,” together with the two closing sentences of that paragraph, now qualifies the claim that every real problem has a workaround. That is the right host because the limit concerns exactly what workaround evidence can and cannot detect.

3. **Population surveys versus customer evidence:** The revised final sentence of the Everpix interpretation now carries the entire qualification. It belongs there because it governs the inference drawn from Everpix’s twelve per cent, not the mechanics of surveying generally.

4. **Perfect interviews with the wrong people:** The revised business-authority sentence now carries the limit and its Cydoc example. Authority is the right host because the failure lies outside the interviewee’s truthful experience—in the other approvals and economics required for a purchase.

## 3. What the chapter loses

The chapter loses the conspicuous pause in which its reservations were gathered and labelled, but none of their argumentative force.
