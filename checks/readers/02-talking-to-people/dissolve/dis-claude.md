    Boundaries
    This case tests the diagnosis, not the cure. Everpix's evidence is surveys

**Where each limit now lives**

1. **Diagnosis, not cure** → the sentence that introduces the method ("So the method reduces to a single instruction"). That is the claim being qualified — the moment the chapter turns from evidence to prescription — so the reservation about the prescription's evidential standing belongs on it, before the reader is handed five questions. The closing "counter-case" section already admits the debt in general terms; this puts it where the promise is made.

2. **No precedent** → question 2's "A problem with no workaround is usually not a problem." This was the sentence that already pointed to the boundary; the limit is the exact "when that is wrong," so it takes the full paragraph in place of the pointer. The decision rule's cross-reference is repaired to a self-contained phrase rather than pointing at a section that no longer exists.

3. **Survey of everybody** → "Set the second and third side by side, because that pair is the finding." The claim is that the *pair* is the finding; the limit is that a single number isn't. Same sentence, stated positively and negatively. The funnel half of the limit was already carried word-for-word by the paid-vs-organic paragraph and is simply deleted.

4. **Wrong people** → question 4, Authority. It is the one question whose whole point is that the interviewee is not the only thing that has to be true, so the Cydoc arithmetic is the reason the question exists rather than a caveat on the method. "So, in a business" ties the instruction to it.

**What the chapter loses:** the one place where the four reservations stood together as a deliberate act of self-restraint — the reader now meets each limit at its point of use but never sees the author line them up and count them.
