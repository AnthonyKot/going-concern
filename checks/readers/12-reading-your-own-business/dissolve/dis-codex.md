2. Where each limit now lives

1. “Publication is not preparation” is already carried in full by the paragraph beginning “One distinction has to be nailed down before it does damage,” especially “The exemption concerns public disclosure, not preparation” and “Nobody escapes producing the numbers.” It should simply disappear from the deleted section.

2. The edited sentence beginning “The Dutch statute is why” now carries the jurisdictional limit. It qualifies the chapter precisely where the statutory explanation begins, before any Dutch size tests are presented as operative facts.

3. The evidentiary limit is already carried more fully under “What this chapter owes”: “the three weekly numbers are reasoning, not evidence” and “Nothing in this book shows a business adopting them and faring better.” Only the obsolete reference to the boundaries section is deleted.

4. The edited sentence beginning “Read together” now carries the interpretive limit. It qualifies the claim for what the three views reveal at the point that claim is made, while preserving the connection to the previous chapter.

3. What the chapter loses

The chapter loses a compact place where a skeptical reader could audit all four reservations at once.
