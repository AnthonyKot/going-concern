## 2. Hosts

**(A)** The edited “He doubled down anyway…” sentence carries the confound. It already introduces the two simultaneous actions, so it is the natural place to prevent the subsequent recovery from being attributed to either one.

**(B)** The edited investment sentence carries the resource disparity. The books, capital and hire now sit inside the account of the recovery they enabled, with the invoice-dependent reader’s different position stated as an equal main clause.

**(C)** The edited “No offer, no price…” sentence carries the prosumer qualification. It describes the cadence itself, so the kind of transaction for which that cadence works belongs there.

**(D)** The edited yield passage carries the expected conversion rate. It places the benchmark beside the hundred-email result before the reader can mistake five customers for failure.

## 3. What the chapter loses

The chapter loses a conspicuous audit point where its reservations can be reviewed together.
