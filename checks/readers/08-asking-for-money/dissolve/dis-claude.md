**2. Hosts**

- **(A)** — "He doubled down anyway, and did two things at once." This is the sentence that asserts the simultaneity; the confound is a property of that fact, so the reservation belongs at the moment the reader learns it, before the figures strip invites them to credit either move.
- **(B)** — the $50,000 / hire sentence. It is the one place the chapter states his cushion as fact; attaching "not on nothing" and the invoice-dependent reader there means the caveat sits immediately above the growth figures it qualifies.
- **(C)** — "money now plausibly on the table." That clause is where the chapter first characterises the transaction the email is for; saying what kind of money (small, personal, one call) is the natural completion of it, and the generalise/cadence split lands before the reader is told whom to email.
- **(D)** — "asked a hundred people and listened to the ones who said no." This sentence already contains the hundred; naming the five and the ninety-five makes the yield concrete at the point where the chapter is claiming the nos are the valuable output — which is exactly the claim a reader with five customers needs to believe.
