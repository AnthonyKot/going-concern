2. **“Could Not Contact” contains unknown outcomes.** No additional host edit is needed. 3. **Most outcomes are harder to measure than a job.** The revised “Define the outcome before the sale…” sentence is the right host because the qualification governs that instruction directly while leaving the instruction intact.

4. **The evidence does not describe the industry.** The revised “Because this is one school's self-published report…” sentence places the voluntary standard, selection effect and one-school scope inside the precise claim the case is being asked to support.
