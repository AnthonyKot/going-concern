## 1. Edits

### Reach and sampling

**BEFORE**

> It measures demand among the people who saw it, and a favour from a stranger determined who saw it.

**AFTER**

> A presale measures demand only among the people who saw it, so a failure can be a reach failure rather than a demand failure: show a preorder page to two hundred people and sell nothing, and you have learned something about two hundred people; a favour from a stranger determined who saw the Glif.

### Basis of the ladder

**BEFORE**

> Evidence begins where saying it starts to cost them.

**AFTER**

> Evidence begins where saying it starts to cost them, which is why the ladder is ordered by what each rung costs the person paying; Studio Neat cannot establish that sequence because it ran one instrument, five times, successfully, not a deposit against a payment against a repeat purchase for the same product, a comparison nobody runs, and its case supports one rung — that a non-refundable payment before existence is obtainable, informative and survivable — not the sequence.

### Independence and customer fit

**BEFORE**

> So rank what you might ask for by what the buyer gives up — not by what it signals, not by how encouraged you feel afterwards.

**AFTER**

> So rank what you might ask for only after independence and fit are established, and then by what the buyer gives up — not by what it signals, not by how encouraged you feel afterwards: a non-refundable payment from someone who is not your customer is worth less than a refundable deposit from someone who is, and the rung is a tiebreak.

## 2. Where each limit now lives

1. **Reach failure:** “A presale measures demand only among the people who saw it…” now carries the limit. It belongs in the paragraph already correcting the interpretation of the Glif result, and it makes the failed-presale case explicit rather than leaving it implied.

2. **Independence and fit:** “So rank what you might ask for only after independence and fit are established…” now carries the limit. This is the chapter’s instruction for using the ladder, so it is the natural place to establish that relevance determines admissibility and the rung only ranks otherwise credible evidence.

3. **The ladder’s evidentiary basis:** “Evidence begins where saying it starts to cost them…” now carries the limit. That sentence introduces the principle behind the ordering, so it should also distinguish the cost-based argument from what the Studio Neat case actually demonstrates.

The chapter loses the quick, labelled summary of its three reservations.
