## Hosts

**(A)** now lives in the "one narrow trade" paragraph. That is the only place the chapter names the tactic as a tactic rather than as his history, and the paragraph's own point — that the skill was narrow, not "marketing in general" — is exactly what makes the closure of the tactic a limit on the case rather than on the argument. (The "algorithm changes that later closed this tactic" clause in the question-two paragraph already prepares the reader; no change needed there.)

**(B)** now lives in question one, "Are the right people there?" The teachers-in-Google fact is stated there as the thing he found, so the reservation that most businesses will not find it attaches to the claim at the moment it is made, and the list of substitute assets arrives before the Monday item that sends the reader to find "the forum, the association, the newsletter."

**(C)** now lives in question four. The 24-cents-versus-2-cents line is the chapter's arithmetic in its purest form, and the limit is a limit on that arithmetic; putting it there also means the reader meets it before the "Do the two-cent arithmetic" Monday instruction, rather than after.

## What the chapter loses

The one place where its three reservations could be read together, as a single honest inventory of what the case cannot carry — now the reader collects them in passing and has to assemble the inventory themselves.
