2. Hosts

(A) “The distinction that holds is narrower…” now carries the limit. It is the right host because it defines the durable principle and can distinguish that principle directly from the obsolete tactic.

(B) “Thousands of teachers a day were typing…” now carries the limit. It supports the first channel test—whether the right people are present—and therefore belongs beside the claim that search demand existed.

(C) “For this low-price impulse purchase…” now carries the limit. It qualifies the cost-per-opportunity arithmetic at the point where that arithmetic is introduced.

3. What the chapter loses

The chapter loses a conspicuous, easily scanned statement of its domain of applicability.
