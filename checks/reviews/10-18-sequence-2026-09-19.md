## Exercise walkthrough and remaining limits

Walked the exercises with the supplied figures and the missing-data paths. The sequence can be
followed without completing a later chapter's exercise prematurely. Unknown customer outcomes,
unagreed receipts, missing household history and an absent handover recipient remain visible.
The optional stock example requires no subscription/retention fiction for anonymous buyers.

The wide shared CSV and duplicate detail prompts in the business-review sheet could still be
made easier to use. Current chapter instructions explain staged use; this is residual usability
debt, not evidence that the calculations require all fields immediately. No user test was run.

Progress to chapter 19 is justified after these corrections. Retain the required external review
before publication. The next review should test whether a reader can apply the instructions to
their own records, rather than only follow the supplied arithmetic.
