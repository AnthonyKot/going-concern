## Worked checks and uncertainty

- Draft due 11:00; internal response due 10:00. A 11:15 response misses both.
- Customer delivery at 12:40 misses the original noon promise but meets revised 13:00.
- Earlier delivery counts: 3 on time + 1 late + 1 unknown = 5. Actual on-time count could be
  3 or 4, so the next window's 4/5 is not proof of improvement.
- Later counts: 4 on time + 1 late = 5. Corrections stay 1/5 in each window; the example
  explicitly assumes completed seven-day follow-up and available correction records for all ten.
- Combined coordination time: 95 − 75 = 20 extra minutes; a two-person eight-minute call
  consumes sixteen participant-minutes. Substantive production/checking is excluded consistently.
- Review is due after two weeks even if work/follow-up is incomplete; partial evidence remains
  partial rather than postponing the review indefinitely.

All figures and outcomes in the Maya/Sam scenario are invented. No before/after trial was run
in a real business. Differences in workload, the unknown timestamp and small counts limit inference.
